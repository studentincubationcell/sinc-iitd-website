# SInC Design Mood Board

Patterns extracted from multi-site research (patterns only — no clones).

## Hero & typography
- **YC / Antler**: Confident oversized headlines, founder-first copy
- **Linear / Vercel**: Tight tracking, product-grade sans (Geist)

## Scroll & motion
- **Composio / Odyssey case studies**: Chapter-by-chapter scroll reveals
- **Apple product pages**: Sticky side content + scrolling cards
- **Lenis**: Buttery smooth scroll wrapper

## Layout
- **Raycast / Stripe**: Bento grids, gradient meshes, asymmetric sections
- **Harvard i-Lab**: Stats + clear pathways to Apply

## Events & community
- **Luma**: Date badges, category pills, clean event cards

## SInC execution
- Bold electric blue + IIT maroon + gold accents
- Full-viewport cinematic intro
- Each homepage section = visual chapter with color shift
- Empty states that still feel premium

## Anti-references
- devclub.in — generic stacked template
- sovarias.in — off limits (senior's site)
